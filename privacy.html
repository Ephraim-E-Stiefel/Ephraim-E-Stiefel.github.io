Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

AIS-OS Workspace is a private tool used only by its owner, Ephraim Stiefel, with his own Google account. No one else uses it.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent, it accesses his own Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Contacts and Chat. It reads data, creates drafts and edits files on his request. It does not send email on his behalf.

How the data is used

Data is used only to help the owner with his own work. It is processed on his own computer and by the AI model provider he uses (Anthropic) when he asks his assistant to work with it. It is not sold, not shared with any other party and not used for advertising.

Storage and deletion

Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
